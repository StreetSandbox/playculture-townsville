Location: Townsville (meetups vary) check out Facebook group for event locations and times
Requirements: smartphone, lungs to run, most importantly, to have fun!
What we're about: A local initiative bringing people together through gamified fitness. We host real-life stealth and chase games, engineering classic tag into an adrenaline-fueled experience built for adults.
How it works: Sessions take place in custom arenas; a set radius expanding from a central zone or custom 'maps'. Players navigate the real-world boundary using the PlayCulture mobile app to locate tactical extraction zones, secure vital health pickups, and access the in-game shop while completely avoiding physical contact from the zombies.
